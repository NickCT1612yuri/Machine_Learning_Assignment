\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Motivation and assignment alignment}

The Machine Learning course assignment requires a full pipeline: exploratory data analysis (EDA), preprocessing, feature extraction, model training, and evaluation. Task~2 focuses on text data and mandates (i)~a traditional pipeline with configurable preprocessing and classifiers, (ii)~at least two feature extractors spanning lexical and modern embedding approaches, with embeddings saved as \texttt{.npy} or \texttt{.h5}, and (iii)~an EDA report plus a comparison of traditional and modern methods. An optional deep-learning pipeline earns bonus credit when compared against the traditional path.

Rather than concentrating on a single corpus, we deliberately contrast \textbf{three} public datasets so that hypotheses about lexical overlap, compositionality, and fine-grained intent can be tested under matched experimental protocol. The same fit~/~validation~/~test discipline, validation Macro-F1 selection, and metric suite apply to every corpus.

\subsection{Research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{When do lexical features suffice?} On topic classification (AG News), does TF-IDF with a linear or Naive Bayes head remain competitive with frozen sentence embeddings?
  \item \textbf{When do contrastive embeddings help?} On Rotten Tomatoes sentiment, does frozen BGE-small improve over bag-of-$n$-grams when polarity depends on composition rather than topic nouns?
  \item \textbf{What happens under fine-grained intent?} On Banking77 (77 classes), how do sparse TF-IDF, frozen BGE, and a small TextCNN trade off Macro-F1 and calibration?
  \item \textbf{Beyond accuracy:} Do Brier/ECE and a negation split reveal reliability gaps that Macro-F1 alone hides?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{itemize}
  \item A single entrypoint (\texttt{assignment\_pipeline.py}) covering three corpora, TF-IDF/BGE feature persistence, TextCNN, and validation Macro-F1 selection without test leakage.
  \item Cross-corpus comparison of traditional (TF-IDF), modern (frozen BGE-small), and bonus deep learning (TextCNN).
  \item Light academic extras: multiclass Brier score, top-label ECE, and negation-cue accuracy splits on the test set.
  \item An HCMUT-formatted LaTeX report whose numbers are taken from regenerated \texttt{outputs/} artefacts.
\end{itemize}
