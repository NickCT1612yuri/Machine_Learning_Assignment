\section{Conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{On Banking77, traditional TF-IDF + LinearSVC is the strongest configuration studied} (89.18 Macro-F1), ahead of frozen DistilBERT + LogReg (87.29) and Word2Vec + BiLSTM (83.07).
  \item \textbf{Preprocessing is representation-dependent.} Stop-word removal harms TF-IDF and DistilBERT. Subword tokenization and masked padding are critical for DistilBERT (+14.4~/~+18.3 Macro-F1). Negation marking improves Word2Vec + BiLSTM by +4.13 Macro-F1.
  \item \textbf{Bonus BiLSTM occupies a clear niche} over mean-pooled Word2Vec (+4.6 Macro-F1 vs.\ LogReg) with strong calibration, but does not replace sparse linear models on this corpus.
  \item \textbf{Errors concentrate in near-synonymous intent families}, and negated queries remain harder across methods.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Limitations and future work.} Results use a single seed and fixed hyperparameters. DistilBERT is frozen; end-to-end fine-tuning or contrastive sentence encoders would likely change the traditional--modern ranking. The \texttt{padding=False} condition jointly disables length and masking.
