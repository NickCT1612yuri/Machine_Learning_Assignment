\section{Conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Cross-corpus contrast matters.} TF-IDF, frozen BGE-small, and TextCNN change relative ranking across AG News, Rotten Tomatoes, and Banking77; no single family dominates all three under our protocol.
  \item \textbf{Traditional vs.\ modern.} Sparse TF-IDF remains highly competitive on lexical topic classification, while frozen contrastive embeddings are especially useful for compositional sentiment and fine-grained intent.
  \item \textbf{Bonus TextCNN} provides an end-to-end neural baseline with probability estimates, but---trained from scratch---trails stronger feature+linear pipelines when labelled data per class is limited.
  \item \textbf{Brier, ECE, and negation} add academic depth beyond Macro-F1 and highlight reliability gaps on negated queries.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Limitations and future work.} Results use a single seed and one validation split without confidence intervals. BGE is frozen; task-specific fine-tuning or SetFit-style adaptation would likely change rankings. TextCNN hyperparameters were not exhaustively tuned. Older Banking77-only artefacts under \texttt{reports/} (if present) are historical and are not claimed as results of this pipeline.
